# Wild Kit

Weekend projects for wild little kits.

**Kids invent it. Parents print it. Saturday happens.**

Wild Kit Co. is a family studio. The website is the studio door: about us, the purpose, the apps, where to get them, and what is coming. Saturday Jobs live in the kits. The first listing is **Lemonade Stand by Wild Kit**. Rascal is the face. Always titled `[Job] by Wild Kit`.

The app is not the product. The driveway artifact is the product.

## Lemonade Stand

- Menu of three to six things
- Mix a special pitcher
- Logo sticker
- Yard poster, letter or 11×17, fill the sheet
- Price cards on a letter sheet
- Supply checklist from the house

- Till, tally, close the day
- Parent Desk for print and zip

## Money

Free invent + print-your-own file. First dollar is the poster pack. Wild Kit Family ($4.99/mo or $29/yr) waits. No ads. No kid payments in v1.

## What we will not do

- Kid Instagram, kid email, kid-to-stranger chat
- Venmo in v1
- A chore chart, a lemonade game, or babysitter TV
- Selling this next to On This Water. Different company.
- The word “coon.” A trash-can joke for Rascal.

## Print

Parent Desk. Email and zip. Letter or 11×17, 300 dpi, fill the sheet. PDF / PNG now. Shop send is v2.

Copy `.env.example` to `.env.local` for Resend. Do not send Wild Kit mail from another company’s domain.
