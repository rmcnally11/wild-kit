# Wild Kit Co. — Business plan

September 2026. Studio door: getwildkit.com

## Thesis

Most family apps keep Saturday on the phone. Wild Kit is a studio of weekend jobs kids invent and parents make real. First listing: Lemonade Stand by Wild Kit. Then Bake Sale, Car Wash, Blanket Fort, and the rest of the shelf.

Wild kids. Hands-on parents.
Kids invent it. Parents make it real. Saturday happens.

The artifact you can hold is the product. The app is the workshop.

## What it is

- Company: Wild Kit Co.
- Developer name: Wild Kit
- Series: Saturday Jobs
- Face: Rascal
- Category: Lifestyle 4+. Not Kids unless entered on purpose.
- Account: parent-owned. First name only. No kid inbox. No ads.

## Who

Parent 28–45 with kids roughly 5–11. House already loud on Saturday morning. They will not download a chore chart. They will print a crooked sign if the kid named the stand.

Kid is the boss of inventing. Parent brings the missing piece. Then everybody leaves the phone.

## Market

Crowded room: kids apps, subscription boxes, activity printables, parental-control tools. Empty chair: combined-effort Saturday job that ends outside.

Do not compete with babysitter TV. Do not become STEM homework.

## Product

1. Invent. Kid designs name, logo, menu, prices. Three templates.
2. Make it real. Printer, oven, hose, blankets. Changes by job.
3. Open. Then leave the phone.

First ship: Lemonade Stand by Wild Kit.
First dollar: $9 print pack (PDF first).
Family: $4.99 / mo or $29 / yr.
App free. No ads. No Venmo. No kid payments.

## Jobs

Lemonade Stand, Bake Sale, Car Wash, Blanket Fort, Birdhouse, Garden Box, Neighborhood Newspaper, Pet Parade, Treasure Map, Garage Sale, Puppet Theater, Backyard Olympics.

Do not build twelve apps before one real Saturday exists.

## 90 days

1–30: domain live. Apple Developer as Wild Kit. One real lemonade Saturday. Photograph the table. App Store Connect shell. Do not submit yet.
31–60: TestFlight. Privacy labels. Print-pack checkout. Three families besides the house.
61–90: Submit Lemonade Stand by Wild Kit. Lifestyle 4+. Bake Sale only after one opened stand.

## Will not

Kid social. Kid email. Third-party ads. A lemonade simulator. A payments app. A fifth slogan.
