# Talk like a person in the driveway

Wild Kit language kit. September 2026.

Same job as Talk Like a Person on the Dock. Different room. You are a dad talking to another dad who is already tired. You are not a brand. You are not a teacher. You are not Pinkfong.

## The mouth

Second person. Contractions. One breath. One idea.

Kid screens: short commands.
Parent screens: calm. No TED talk.
Rascal: already written in lib/rascal-hints.ts. Do not get cuter than that.

## Spine — keep these

Kids invent it. Parents make it real. Saturday happens.
Weekend projects for wild little kits.
The poster is the product.
Grown-up first. Then you invent it.
Parent buys. Kid uses.
First name only. No kid inbox.
You opened. That's the whole point.
Pack from the house. Then go outside.
Crooked is fine.
Always titled [Job] by Wild Kit.

## Who is speaking

### To a parent

You hit print. You keep the account. You do not have to be crafty.
The kid names it. You run the printer. Then you stand there.
No kid inbox. No birthday. No street address.
If the poster is crooked, it still works.

Do not say: empower, screen-time solution, educational outcomes, STEM, curriculum, users, platform, experience.

### To a kid

You set the price.
You're the boss of this stand.
Name it whatever you want.
Draw it however you want.
Another cup. Go.

Do not say: great job buddy, let's learn about money, you are an entrepreneur.
Do not grade them. Do not clap.

### Rascal

Tiny foreman. One breath.
Never a teacher. Never a trash-can joke.
If it sounds like a mascot at a bank, throw it out.
Use the pool. Do not invent a funnier raccoon.

## THE APPROACH

Open with the Saturday, not the app.

"We printed a poster last night. They ran a stand till the pitcher was gone."

You have given him a picture. He will ask what you used. Then:

"Kid invents the menu. You hit print. Then you go outside. That's the whole app."

Generous. Accurate. No pitch deck.
Do not quote a subscription on a first mention.
Do not compare it to Pinkfong out loud. That is the studio model, not the sentence.

## The line that matters

Telling another parent what you did Saturday is a conversation.
Calling this an educational product is a claim you do not want to make.

G-rated. Combined effort. Nothing crazy.
